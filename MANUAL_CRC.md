# ALTESA Clinical App - User Manual (CRC View)

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to the Clinical Research Coordinator (CRC) manual for the **ALTESA** clinical trial platform. This application is designed to guide coordinators through standard operating procedures, timeline compliance, and protocol adherence in a secure, privacy-first manner.

## 2. Activation & Security Initialization
ALTESA operates on a zero-trust model. The app must be activated before any CRC can use it.
1. **Manager Bootstrapping:** The Clinical Trial Manager must first initialize the study from the Manager Portal using the Master Protocol Config.
2. **Device Activation:** Once initialized, a site-specific **Device Activation Code** is generated by the Manager.
3. **Setup PIN:** The CRC must enter the Activation Code and choose a secure 4-8 digit PIN. This PIN encrypts local session data.
4. **Login:** After activation, the CRC will log in using this PIN. The application automatically locks after a period of inactivity to prevent unauthorized access.

## 3. Global Dashboard Overview
Once logged in, the CRC lands on the main **Dashboard**.
- **Metrics Bar:** At the top, you'll see quick counts of total patients, critical cases, and warnings.
- **Daily Briefing (Clipboard Icon):** A modal detailing immediate tasks due today, time-critical randomizations, and warnings.
- **Notifications (Bell Icon):** A centralized feed of alerts (e.g., "Patient 102 missed their 48h randomization window").
- **Search (Magnifying Glass):** Opens the Command Palette to quickly search for any patient across the site using their ID.
- **Filter Tabs (All, Critical, Action Needed, Routine):** Allow you to filter the patient roster by urgency. Urgent patients (red) and warnings (yellow) are automatically pushed to the top.
- **View Modes (Grid, List, Calendar):** Toggle the presentation of your patients. The **Calendar** view is extremely useful for planning clinic visits based on the protocol schedule.

## 4. Patient Management
### 4.1 Adding a Patient
1. Click the **"Add Patient"** button.
2. Provide the Subject ID, Name, Language, Consent Date, and assign the appropriate **Protocol Amendment**.
3. *Note on Protocol Version:* The rules applied to the patient will depend on the amendment version you select.

### 4.2 Editing a Patient
1. Open the patient's file.
2. Click the **"Edit Details"** button (pencil icon).
3. Update demographics or change their assigned Protocol version. Modifying the protocol version will trigger a recalculation of their upcoming timeline tasks.

## 5. Subject Detail & Timeline
When you open a patient, you arrive at the **Patient Detail View**. This is the core operating interface of ALTESA.

### 5.1 The Timeline Pipeline
The patient's journey is mapped vertically:
- **Tasks (Nodes):** Check-ups, questionnaires, samples.
- **Dependency Lines:** Arrows connect tasks that are prerequisites for later steps. 
- **States:** 
  - *Orange:* Locked/Blocked (prerequisites not met)
  - *Blue:* Available and active
  - *Green (Checkmarked):* Completed

### 5.2 Completing Tasks
1. Click on any blue (Available) task.
2. You will see a checklist or a form.
3. Once all requirements are fulfilled, toggle the task status to **Completed**.
*Warning:* Completing a task immediately unlocks any downstream tasks that depend on it.

### 5.3 Modifying Status & Overrides
In some cases, a protocol deviation occurs (e.g., a test was skipped for clinical safety). 
You may override the status of a task by entering a specific reason. This is permanently logged in the **Compliance Vault**.

## 6. Time-Critical Randomization 
The ALTESA protocol demands randomisation within **48 hours (+ 6 hours grace period)** of RV symptom onset.
1. When a patient reports positive symptoms (DTQ task), a **Count-Down Timer** appears prominently at the top of their profile.
2. To randomize the patient, you must trigger the **Randomisation Wizard**.
3. **Wizard Guardrails:** The wizard will forcefully check if Prerequisites (like positive DTQ and ICF) are met. You cannot proceed unless all critical checklist items are confirmed.

## 7. Study Outcomes
- **Screening Success / Failure:** If a patient is deemed ineligible before randomisation, open the Screening Task and flag it as "Screen Failure." Supply the required rationale. The patient is archived but remains in the audit log.
- **Study Completion:** Toggling the final task closes the patient's active loop.

## 8. Compliance & Audit Vault
- Access the **Vault** via the shield icon or Data menu.
- **Archive:** View past subjects.
- **Audit Logs:** A tamper-evident cryptographic log of *every* action performed across the device (e.g., "User X completed Task Y for Patient Z on Timestamp"). This log is mathematically sealed and can be provided to monitors for CFR Part 11 auditing. 

## 9. Help & Shortcuts
At any time, press `Cmd/Ctrl + K` to open search, or click the **Help (?)** button in the dashboard for a quick glossary of terms like "RV Onset", "PSB Window", and keyboard shortcuts.
